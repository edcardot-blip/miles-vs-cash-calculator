    How this calculation works

    What counts as cash cost. Fare × travelers, plus any checked-bag fees not waived by your cabin, status, or credit card. Bag benefits don't stack — you get whichever single free-bag allowance is largest, not all of them added together.

    What counts as award cost. Miles required × travelers, plus award taxes/carrier-imposed fees × travelers, plus the same bag-fee logic as the cash side (status/card bag waivers apply to award tickets too).

    How AA flight miles are calculated Objective formula

    base fare × (1 − est. govt. tax %) × 5 miles/$ × (1 + status bonus %). American earns AAdvantage miles on the base fare only, excluding government-imposed taxes — we estimate that tax portion at 7.5% by default (adjustable in Advanced mode) since it isn't broken out in the ticket price you enter. Basic Economy tickets purchased on or after Dec 17, 2025 earn $0.

    How Loyalty Points are calculated Objective formula

    1 Loyalty Point per redeemable mile earned from flying — this includes your elite status bonus (not just the base 5/$), since the bonus isn't a "promotional" mile. From credit-card spend, only the card's base 1×/$ counts toward Loyalty Points — the accelerated 2×/3×/4× category bonus does not.

    How credit-card rewards are calculated Objective formula

    Card miles = total cash outlay (fare + bags, since it's all charged to the card) × the card's AA-purchase multiplier. This is separate from — and additive with — AAdvantage flight miles, not a replacement for them.

    How baggage is calculated

    Domestic U.S. pricing only (v1 scope): $45/$55 online (1st/2nd bag), +$5 at the airport, +$5 more on Basic Economy, flat $200 for a 3rd+ bag. Free-bag allowances come from your cabin, AAdvantage status, or credit card — whichever is greatest.

    How opportunity cost affects the result Assumption-driven

    "Effective value" nets the forgone AAdvantage miles, Loyalty Points, and credit-card rewards (all priced using your two valuation sliders) out of the raw cash-vs-miles gap, then divides by the miles required. That's the number compared against your personal mile valuation to produce the recommendation.

    What's user-controlled vs. fixed

    Objective: mileage formulas, elite bonus percentages, Loyalty Point thresholds, published bag fees, the taxes/miles/fare you enter. Assumption: what a mile and a Loyalty Point are worth to you, and the government-tax share of the fare.

    Not yet modeled

    • Partner/codeshare-operated flights (distance-based earning, different fuel-surcharge rules — especially British Airways/Iberia)
    • International award taxes and bag rules (this build is domestic U.S. only)
    • Seat-selection or upgrade-certificate pricing
    • Promotional/targeted bonus mile offers, sign-up bonuses, or elite-qualifying dollar (EQD)-style requirements beyond Loyalty Points
    • The Citi AAdvantage Executive card's $150K/year 5× spend tier (modeled at its base 4×)
